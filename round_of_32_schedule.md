## Tournament Notes

* FIFA World Cup 2026 is the first World Cup featuring 48 teams.
* The knockout stage begins with a Round of 32.
* The Round of 32 consists of:

  * 12 Group Winners
  * 12 Group Runners-up
  * 8 Best Third-Place Teams
* Winners advance to the Round of 16.
